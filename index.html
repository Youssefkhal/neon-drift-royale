HOW TO PLAY

Last ship alive wins. The zone shrinks every 20 seconds; outside it you lose HP.

MOVE WASD / arrows   AIM mouse   FIRE hold click or SPACE
UPGRADES press 1 / 2 / 3 or tap a card when XP levels you up
MAP TAB or M   PAUSE ESC

Kill drones and asteroids for XP and orbs. Hit players for XP too. Green + pickups heal inside the zone.

Touch: left half drags a stick to move, right half aims and fires.
